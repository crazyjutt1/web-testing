Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the Colour Picker website uses cookies and similar browser storage.

No tracking cookies

The Colour Picker does not use tracking or advertising cookies. There is no analytics script collecting behaviour data on this version of the site.

LocalStorage, not cookies

To remember your colour history and favourite colours, the Colour Picker uses your browser's LocalStorage rather than cookies. LocalStorage data:

  • Is stored only on your own device
  • Is never sent to a server as part of normal browsing
  • Can be cleared through your browser's site data settings

Essential functionality only

Any browser storage used by the Colour Picker exists solely to make the tool more convenient — remembering favourites and recent picks — and is never used for advertising or profiling.

Managing your data

You can clear the Colour Picker's saved history and favourites at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this page, or by using the Reset button inside the tool.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if the Colour Picker's storage practices change in the future.

Contact us

Questions about this Cookie Policy can be sent via the contact section of the Colour Picker website.
