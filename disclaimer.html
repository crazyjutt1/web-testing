Colour accuracy

The Colour Picker calculates HEX, RGB, HSL, HSV and CMYK values using standard formulas, but how a colour actually appears can vary between monitors, printers and devices. The Colour Picker cannot guarantee that a colour will look identical on every screen or in print.

CMYK approximation

The CMYK values shown by the Colour Picker are a mathematical approximation intended for general reference. For professional print work, always confirm final colours with your printer or a colour-managed proofing process.

No professional advice

Information on the Colour Picker website, including the Formats Guide and FAQ, is provided for general informational purposes and does not constitute professional design or print consulting advice.

Eyedropper feature

The "Pick Colour From Screen" feature depends on your browser supporting the EyeDropper API. The Colour Picker cannot control browser compatibility and displays a fallback message when this feature is unavailable.
