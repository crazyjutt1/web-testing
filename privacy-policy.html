Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Colour Picker website handles information when you use it. The Colour Picker is designed to run entirely inside your browser, and this policy describes exactly what that means for your data.

Information the Colour Picker does not collect

The Colour Picker does not require an account, does not ask for your name or email to use the tool, and does not transmit the colours you pick to any server. There is no backend processing behind the Colour Picker's core functionality.

Local Storage

To remember your colour history and favourite colours between visits, the Colour Picker uses your browser's LocalStorage. This information:

  • Stays on your device and is never uploaded anywhere
  • Is only accessible to the Colour Picker website in your own browser
  • Can be cleared at any time through your browser settings

Contact form

The contact form on the Colour Picker website performs validation only in your browser. It is not connected to a backend or email-sending service in this version of the site.

Cookies

The Colour Picker does not set tracking cookies. See our Cookie Policy for further detail.

Third-party links

The Colour Picker's footer may link to external sites (such as social media). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party destinations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the Colour Picker evolves. Continued use of the Colour Picker after changes means you accept the revised policy.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how the Colour Picker works, reach out via the contact section.
